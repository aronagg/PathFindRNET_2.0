# Reviewer Response Line-Number Workflow

1. Format the revised manuscript in the target DOCX template.
2. Enable line numbering according to journal instructions.
3. For each response in `docs/response_to_reviewers_full_draft.md`, identify the
   revised manuscript section and line span.
4. Replace every placeholder:

   `[Section X, Lines YY-ZZ to be filled after DOCX formatting]`

   with the final section and line numbers.
5. Cross-check `docs/response_to_reviewers_matrix.csv` against the full response.
6. Ensure every major reviewer topic has:
   - short response;
   - what changed;
   - evidence/result;
   - caveat where needed;
   - manuscript location.
7. Do not invent line numbers before final DOCX formatting.
