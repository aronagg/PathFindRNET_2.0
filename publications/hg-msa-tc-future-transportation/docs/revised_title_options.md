# Revised Title Options

## Recommended Title

From Geometric Endpoint Micro-Modes to Semantic Maneuver Graphs:
Homography-Guided Vehicle Trajectory Clustering at Complex Urban Intersections

## Alternative Options

1. Homography-Guided Semantic Maneuver Graphs for Leakage-Controlled Vehicle
   Trajectory Clustering
2. Semantic Maneuver Graph Priors for Homography-Guided Vehicle Trajectory
   Clustering at Urban Intersections
3. Leakage-Controlled Evaluation of Homography-Guided Maneuver-Structure Priors
   for Vehicle Trajectory Clustering
4. From Endpoint Micro-Modes to Maneuver Graph Priors in Unsupervised Vehicle
   Trajectory Clustering
5. Homography-Guided Maneuver-Structure Priors and Independent Rule-Based
   Evaluation for Traffic Video Trajectory Clustering

## Recommendation

Use the first title if the journal allows a longer descriptive title. It clearly
signals the revised novelty boundary: the paper is not claiming novelty for
endpoint detection alone, but for the progression from endpoint micro-modes to
semantic maneuver graph priors and independent evaluation.
