# Final Title Recommendation

Recommended title:

**From Geometric Endpoint Micro-Modes to Semantic Maneuver Graphs:
Homography-Guided Vehicle Trajectory Clustering at Complex Urban Intersections**

## Rationale

This title matches the revised novelty boundary. It does not claim novelty for
endpoint detection alone, homography alone or generic trajectory clustering.
Instead, it emphasizes the progression from geometric endpoint micro-modes to
semantic maneuver graph priors and the application to complex urban
intersections.

## Shorter Backup Title

**Homography-Guided Semantic Maneuver Graphs for Vehicle Trajectory Clustering at
Urban Intersections**

Use the shorter option only if the journal strongly prefers shorter titles.
